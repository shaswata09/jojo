# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project about something stressful — looking
for work — and there is no version of a technical disagreement that is worth
being unkind over.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith.
- Criticise code, decisions and ideas; not people.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and that this is not a
  judgement about you.
- Respect that people contribute in the time they have.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind, including: personal attacks, demeaning comments about
someone's identity or background, unwelcome sexual attention, deliberate
misgendering, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of
discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project
is represented.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub's private reporting, or by opening an issue
asking to be contacted privately. Reports will be handled discreetly.
Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions, and may block accounts, where
that is the proportionate response.

Maintainers are held to the same standard as everyone else, and more visibly.
